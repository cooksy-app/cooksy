## 📱 Android

1. On your phone, tap **[Download Cooksy.apk](https://github.com/cooksy-app/cooksy/releases/latest/download/Cooksy.apk)**.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to **install unknown apps** (Settings → Apps → your browser → Install unknown apps).
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

New versions: download the latest APK again and install it over the old one. Your data is kept.

## 🍎 iPhone & iPad

Apple doesn't allow installing apps from a download file, so Cooksy runs from Safari:

1. Open **[cooksy-app.github.io/cooksy](https://cooksy-app.github.io/cooksy/)** in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (square with arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

It then opens full-screen from your home screen, like a normal app, and works offline.

## 💻 Laptop / desktop

Open **[cooksy-app.github.io/cooksy](https://cooksy-app.github.io/cooksy/)** in any browser. In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar to add it as an app.

## What you can do

- **Allergy-aware recipes.** Choose allergens and ingredients to avoid; recipes that contain them are filtered out everywhere. Gluten-free mode flags packaged ingredients that need a gluten-free label.
- **Cuisines A–Z.** Over 100 food traditions, from Pakistani and Indian to Hungarian, Ethiopian and Thai.
- **My Kitchen.** Track what's in your fridge, pantry and freezer, with expiry dates.
- **Cook with what I have.** See which recipes you can make right now.
- **Shopping list.** Add only the missing ingredients, grouped by dish.

> ⚠️ **Always check labels.** Cooksy checks the ingredients listed in each recipe. It can't see inside packaged products, so no recipe is guaranteed allergen-free. Nutrition values are estimates.

Your profile and lists are stored only on your device.
