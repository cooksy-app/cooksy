Download Cooksy

Pick your device. It’s free, with no ads.

Your device

Android

Download APKAndroid 7.0 or newer
  1. Tap Download APK on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway.

Updating? Install the new APK over the old one. Your data is kept.

Your device

iPhone & iPad

Open Cooksy in SafariThen add it to your Home Screen
  1. Tap the button above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (iPad: top right).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  4. Tap Add. Cooksy now opens from your Home Screen like any app, even offline.

Apple only allows App Store downloads on iPhone, so Cooksy installs from Safari instead.

On a computer? You can also use Cooksy in your browser.
